How the orientation was derived

Facing is the CDIP MOP shore normal at the transect the nearshore forecast already uses for the spot, averaged with its neighbours over about ±200 m of coast (closer transects weigh more). The ±500 m and ±1 km averages are only a stability check.

Offshore wind comes from facing + 180°. Swell window is the open water within ±90° of facing, from rays every 2° over the Salix elevation grids. Only land within 30 km blocks a ray, and gaps up to 8° (a small island's shadow) are bridged. Channel Islands shadowing is not modelled, and swell that wraps around a point from outside the window is not seen: point breaks need a hand-set window.

Anything entered in the rating columns of locations.csv overrides these values. Tide is not part of the hourly score yet, so its factor is always 1.00.
